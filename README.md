# HACKERCARD

Hackercard is a pocket-sized, NFC-powered card I built to make sharing my socials effortless. Instead of fumbling usernames or long URLs, a simple tap transfers your chosen links like - Instagram , Twitter, LinkedIn, Portfolio website and Github directly to the recipient’s phone without apps or typing. It's fast, battery-free, and designed for real moments at meetups, conferences, or casual introductions. Setup is minimal: you choose the links to embed and can update them remotely when needed, so the card stays current without reprinting. I focused on reliability and privacy: only the links you pick are shared, and nothing else. Keep it in your wallet, tap to share, and move on. Easy to carry and fits perfectly in your wallet. Has a cool silkscreen design and my name too on it.
